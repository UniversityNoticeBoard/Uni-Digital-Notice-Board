Examination Schedules

Information regarding mid-term and final examinations

Mid-Term Examinations

Mid-term examinations for all undergraduate programs will commence from 10th March and continue until 18th March.

  • Duration: 90 minutes per paper
  • Admit cards mandatory
  • Reporting time: 30 minutes before exam

Final Examinations

Final examinations are scheduled to begin in the last week of May. The detailed timetable will be uploaded separately.
